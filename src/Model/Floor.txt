10|The player wakes up alone on floor 10 after a long coma, with no memory of how they got there. The floor is dark and silent, with dusty furniture, abandoned belongings, and old supplies scattered throughout the hallway.
9|Deep scratches cover the walls and doors throughout the floor. Shattered glass covers parts of the hallway, spiders crawl along the walls, and several doors look like something has tried to break through them.
8|Red lights flicker throughout the floor while smoke slowly slips out from underneath some of the doors. The air smells burnt, and black marks stretch across the walls and ceiling from an old fire.
7|Water drops constantly from the ceiling and large puddles cover the hallway. Wet footprints lead between some of the rooms, but strangely, there is never anyone there when the plater follow them.
6|Wines and roots have grown through cracks in the walls and spread across the floor. Some doors are partly covered by them, while bugs and strange insects crawl between the plants and disappear into the rooms.
5|Old newspapers, photographs, and hotel records are scattered across the hallway. some mention missing guests and unexplained incidents, giving the player the first real clues about what happened before the hotel was abandoned.
4|Thick fog fills the entire floor, making the doors difficult to see from a distance. Shadows occasionally move through the fog, and strange noises seem to come from different rooms every few seconds.
3|Strange symbols and handprints cover the walls and doors. Candles line parts of the hallway, and some suddenly light on their own as the player walks past them.
2|This floor looks like someone desperately tried to keep something trapped inside. Some doors are barricaded with furniture, warning messages are written across the walls, and loud banging can be heard from inside the rooms.
1|The hallway ends at a single set of double doors, heavier than any other on the way down. A brass nameplate reads "Manager" in handwriting that doesn't match the rest of the hotel's signage. The air is still like the floor has been holding its breath since Floor 10.
